## Design System

- **Zero JavaScript:** Built completely with pure semantic HTML5 and `style.css`.
- **Palette:** Warm off-white paper (`#f8f8f2`), deep forest-slate contrast (`#101c1e`), chartreuse (`#d4f34a`), coral (`#ee7256`), and teal (`#33a598`).
- **Typography:** Fraunces (editorial serif), DM Sans (body), and Space Mono (metadata & technical tags).
- **Responsive Layout:** CSS Grid and Flexbox with breakpoints at 1024px, 768px, and 480px.
- **Accessibility & Motion:** Semantic landmarks, skip-link, high contrast ratios, and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
